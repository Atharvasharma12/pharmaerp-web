Main flow (recommended)

Lead Created (CRM)

Source: website form / referral / call / LinkedIn

Qualification (CRM)

Decide: fit or not fit

If not fit → Lost/Closed

Discovery Call / Requirement Gathering (CRM)

Proposal / Quotation (CRM)

Scope + timeline + cost + payment terms

Negotiation / Revision (CRM)

Deal Won (CRM)

Client confirms (email/PO/approval)

NDA + Contract/SOW Signed (Contracts module)

NDA (if needed)

MSA/SOW (usually required)

Client Onboarding (Projects/Operations)

Kickoff meeting

Access setup

Communication channels

Assign team

Project Execution (Projects)

Tasks, milestones, delivery

Invoicing (Invoicing module)
Depending on your billing model:

Advance invoice (before work starts), OR

Milestone-based invoice, OR

Monthly retainer invoice

Payment Received (Invoicing/Finance)

Delivery + Closure (Projects)

Offboarding / Support / AMC (Support module)

Handover docs

Support tickets / maintenance contract
